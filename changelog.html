    • Now
      Built and working

      Seven surfaces, live in the app

      Fluxo has not launched publicly, but these are built and running today.

      • Insights — Account, post and story metrics from Instagram, served from a cache on a fixed refresh schedule rather than a live feed.
      • Automations — Trigger, condition, action rules for DMs and comments. The first matching rule wins and then it stops, so nobody ever gets two replies.
      • Post scheduling — Queue posts and publish them to Instagram through Meta's publishing flow.
      • Inbox — DM and comment conversations in one place.
      • Campaigns — A label, an attribution bucket and a master on/off switch, so you can group rules and see what a push produced. It is not a broadcast tool and it does not send bulk messages.
      • Leads — People captured from comments and DMs by a rule you set, attributed to the post they came from, and held on a retention clock you choose.
      • Reports — The above, summarised.
